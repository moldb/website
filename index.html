The problem

AI is entering the lab faster than anyone can check its work.

Life-science organizations are moving from AI pilots to AI-dependent workflows. The output reads well, but whether it holds up against real biology, real data and real decisions is a different question, one that needs a scientist, a software architect and an R&D leader in the same room.

I have been all three. My value is the judgment to tell where a computational system is reliable, where it needs expert review, and what to change when it falls short.

“AI may have a billion pieces of data at its disposal, but it does not have experience.”
